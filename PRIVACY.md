# Privacy Policy: Hidr

_Last updated: November 24, 2026_

Hidr does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Paid plans are handled by Apple (Safari) or ExtensionPay (Chrome), not by Hidr.

- **No data collection.** The extension has no analytics, tracking or ads. On Safari it makes no network requests of its own.
- **What it stores.** Two settings (on/off, and which option you chose), saved by the browser on your device. Nothing else.
- **Where it runs.** Only on x.com and twitter.com (including mobile.x.com and mobile.twitter.com), and only to stop the swipe-up video feed. It doesn't read, save or send the content of the pages you visit.
- **Permissions.** `storage` (to remember your two settings) and access to the X/Twitter sites listed above.
- **Plans on Safari.** You buy them in the Hidr app through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; the extension only asks the app whether a plan is active.
- **Plans on Chrome.** Payments go through [ExtensionPay](https://extensionpay.com) and Stripe. The extension stores a random ID in your browser's synced storage and sends only that ID to extensionpay.com to check whether a plan is active. The email and payment details you enter at checkout, or when you sign in with "Already paid?", go to ExtensionPay and Stripe, never to Hidr's code. See [ExtensionPay's privacy policy](https://extensionpay.com/privacy) and [Stripe's](https://stripe.com/privacy).

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/DavidAnsc/hidr/issues.
